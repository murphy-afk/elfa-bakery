# Design references

Photos downloaded from public listings (Restaurant Guru, Google Maps, social posts) for internal design study only.

These images are watermarked by third party sites and must not ship in the final website. They exist here so the design can match the real look of the shop.

- interior.jpg: price list board, staff at the counter, main room with pendant lights, seating with wood slats and red stools.
- design.jpg: retail shelf with ice cream freezer, seating area with dark ceiling and wood counter, pastry display with staff.
- food.jpg: cheese pizza, funghi pizza, bruschetta with cured meat, cappuccino with focaccia slices on a marble table.

## What to take from these photos

- Warm yellow and amber walls, red accents, wood paneling, dark marble tables.
- Bright glass counters full of food, product shelves on the walls.
- Illy coffee branding and cappuccino with latte art.
- Golden pizza, focaccia, pastries, gourmet sandwiches.
- Small sidewalk seating, quiet residential street.
- Overall mood: cozy, warm, unpretentious, family run.
